ACET ERP · Procurement Module

Every approval, and where it comes from

One page listing every decision point in procurement: what is being approved, who may approve it, where the item arrives from, and the exact screen the decision is made on. If a control exists in the system, it is on this page.

Two rules hold everywhere.

The rules live under the screens, not on them. Every gate below is enforced in the data layer, so a decision cannot be skipped by opening a different page — a button that should not work does not work, and it says why.

Nobody approves their own work. Where a person could sit on both sides — uploading a deliverable and accepting it, for example — the system refuses and names the conflict.

Procurement planning

Nothing may be procured that is not on an approved plan. Departmental and project plans are separate throughout — different budgets, different reviewers, separate queues — so a project activity can never be signed off as departmental spend or the reverse.

Purchase requisitions

A requisition passes three stations, and a fourth above $10,000. Each has to finish before the next can start, and the requisition names which station it is sitting at.

ApprovalWho decidesWhere it comes fromWhere it is decided
Departmental approval 1 of 3 Department Head Raised by staff at Employee Self-Service › Procurement Request Employee Self-Service › Approvals › Procurement Request Approval
Procurement review 2 of 3 Procurement Arrives once the department head has approved Procurement › Approvals › Purchase Requisitions
Finance approval 3 of 3 Finance Arrives once procurement has reviewed Finance › Approvals › Procurement Requests
Senior management authorisation above $10,000 Senior Management Added automatically for high-value requests, and for any emergency override of the plan Procurement › Approvals › Senior Management Approval
Emergency override Senior Management Requested on a requisition for an urgent need that is not on the approved plan Granted on the requisition, then permanently visible on it — a written justification is mandatory and Audit is notified

Contracts, deliverables and invoices

The money side. An invoice cannot be paid without an accepted deliverable behind it, and a contract cannot quietly grow — every increase is a variation approved by four people.

What sits in the Approvals menu

Eight queues, in the order the work flows. The two requisition stations that belong to other departments live in those departments' own menus, which is why they are not repeated here.

Purchase Requisitions· Sourcing· Departmental Plan Approval· Project Plan Approval· Contract Variations· Deliverable Acceptance· Supplier Registrations· Senior Management Approval

Two live elsewhere on purpose. A requisition's first stop is its own department head, at Employee Self-Service › Approvals › Procurement Request Approval, and its budget check belongs to Finance, at Finance › Approvals › Procurement Requests. Both are part of the same chain — they simply appear in the menu of the person who has to act.

Invoice approvals have no separate queue. All four stations are worked on Procurement › Invoices, where each invoice shows a stage strip and only the button for the stage it is actually at.
