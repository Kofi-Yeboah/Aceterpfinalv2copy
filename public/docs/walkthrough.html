End-to-end walkthrough

Every click, in order, from registering a supplier to closing the contract and rating them. Tick each box as it passes. Roughly 45 minutes at a steady pace.

Plans come in two kinds, and they stay apart the whole way.

Departmental plans are the annual plans each department prepares; project plans belong to an approved project. They are reviewed against different budgets, so each has its own approval queue — Approvals › Departmental Plan Approval and Approvals › Project Plan Approval. Part B walks the departmental one; the project one behaves identically from Purchase Plan › Project Plans.

Before you start. Sign in, then find the name badge at the top right — that's the role switcher. You'll use it constantly: the system refuses to let one person approve their own work, so a single identity cannot walk this through.

Your cast: Ama Darko (requestor & contract coordinator) · James Owusu (department head & supervisor) · Felix Addo (procurement) · Abena Osei (finance) · Nana Adjei (COO).

Part A — Get a supplier onto the list

Step 1 of the flow

A1Procurement

Onboard the supplier

Procurement › Supplier Management › Onboard Firm

Choose Corporate entity. Fill the form. Attach a file against Certificate of Incorporation and Tax Clearance Certificate — any PDF will do — and give each an expiry date. Submit.

Expect: a confirmation screen with a new supplier ID (SUP-1010 or higher) and a note that Procurement must review it before the account goes live.
A2Supplier

Try a banned name, to see screening fire

Register again, but type Global Trade Partners Ltd as the legal name.

Expect: a sanctions warning appears as you type, before you finish the form.
A3Felix — Procurement

Approve the registration

Procurement › Supplier Management › Firms

Find your new supplier — status Pending Onboarding. Open the row menu and click Approve Registration.

Expect: status flips to Active. If documents are missing the button refuses and names them.
A4Abena — Finance

Validate the bank account

Procurement › Supplier Management › open the supplier

In the banking panel, click Validate Banking Details.

Expect: marked validated with Abena's name. As Felix, the same button is greyed out with a reason — that's the separation working.

Part B — Put it on the plan

Step 2 of the flow

B1Ama — Requestor

Add an activity to a departmental plan

Procurement › Purchase Plan › Departmental Plans

Pick a department tab, click Add activity, fill it in and click Save as draft.

Expect: a new PP-2026-NNN row at Draft. Type an estimated value and watch the procurement method suggest itself from the threshold band; override it and the form demands a written justification.
B2Ama — Requestor

Submit it for review

Click Submit on your draft row — or Submit N drafts in the header to send the whole department's drafts at once.

Expect: the status becomes Pending Procurement Review. It is now out of your hands — there is no way to approve your own entry.
B3Felix, then Abena

Clear both review stations

Procurement › Approvals › Departmental Plan Approval

Your activity is waiting in Procurement review. Click Review, then approve. Switch to Abena and clear it from Finance verification.

Expect: the entry only reaches Approved after both stations pass it. Try rejecting with an empty reason — the button stays disabled. Now open Project Plan Approval: your departmental entry is not there, and never will be.

Part C — Raise it and get it approved

Steps 3 and 4

C1Ama — Requestor

Initiate a requisition from the plan

Employee Self-Service › Procurement Plan

In the approved plan items table, click Initiate on a row. The Create Purchase Requisition form opens with the plan details already filled in and locked.

Expect: a read-only panel showing the plan activity, category, cost and budget code — proof the requisition is tied to the plan.
C2Ama — Requestor

Try to submit it half-finished

Without attaching a TOR or specification, click Submit Requisition.

Expect: submission is blocked, with red messages naming the exact missing fields.
C3Ama — Requestor

Complete it properly and submit

Attach a document, fill the delivery timeline (goods) or start/end dates (services), then submit. Note the PR number.

Expect: it appears under Procurement › Purchase Requisitions at Pending Dept Approval, with a 5-step tracker showing who holds it.
C4James — Dept Head

Reject it first, on purpose

Employee Self-Service › Approvals › Procurement Request Approval

Open your requisition, click Reject, and try to confirm with an empty reason. Then type one and confirm.

Expect: confirm is disabled until a reason is typed. Status becomes Rejected with the reason on the record.
C5Ama — Requestor

Correct it and resubmit

Procurement › Purchase Requisitions

Open the rejected requisition and click Resubmit for Approval.

Expect: back to Pending Dept Approval, with the rejection reason and a resubmission count retained. This is the route that used to be a dead end.
C6James — Dept Head

Approve it this time

Same screen, click Approve.

Expect: status becomes Pending Procurement & Finance — both stations at once, labelled PARALLEL.
C7Felix — Procurement

Approve one half of the parallel stage

Procurement › Approvals › Purchase Requisitions

Approve it, then look at the requisition's status.

Expect: it does not advance — it's still waiting on Finance. The screen says the other station hasn't cleared yet.
C8Abena — Finance

Approve the other half

Finance › Approvals › Procurement Requests

Approve it.

Expect: if the value is over $10,000 it moves to Pending Senior Mgmt. If under, it goes straight to Approved.
C9Nana — COO

Give final approval

Procurement › Approvals › Senior Management Approval

Approve it.

Expect: status Approved. Watch what happens next on its own.

Part D — Find a supplier

Steps 5, 6 and 7

D1Felix — Procurement

Confirm the sourcing case created itself

Procurement › Sourcing

Expect: a new case is already in the list, carrying your PR number, description, budget and requestor. Nobody created it. Open it — the stepper is built for that buying method.
D2Felix — Procurement

Try changing the method off-threshold

Click Change Method and pick one that doesn't match the value band.

Expect: a warning showing the correct band, and a justification is required before it will accept the change.
D3Felix — Procurement

Upload the solicitation documents

On the first step, use Upload Document against each required slot. Upload a second version of one of them.

Expect: real files attach, and replacing one keeps the previous version in its history rather than overwriting it.
D4Felix — Procurement

Publish or invite

Open competition: tick the channels and click Publish Now. Limited or RFQ: pick bidders and click Send Invitations.

Expect: in the supplier picker, Kwame Construction Ltd is flagged and needs management sign-off, and blacklisted suppliers can't be selected at all.
D5Procurement

Record a sealed bid

Procurement › Sourcing › case › Bid Opening

Open the tender you just published and click Record Bid Received. Pick the supplier, enter the amount and attach the offer.

Expect: an immediate receipt with a submission ID and acknowledgement reference.
D6Felix — Procurement

Try to open the envelope early

Back in the sourcing case, click Open Technical Envelope without closing the tender first.

Expect: refused, telling you the tender is still open and giving the closing date. The bid shows as Sealed — supplier and timestamp visible, contents not.
D7Felix — Procurement

Close, then open the envelopes in order

Close Tender for Submissions → Open Technical Envelope → Open Financial Envelope.

Expect: bids unseal and auto-populate the bid list. Who opened each envelope and when is recorded.
D8Felix — Procurement

Evaluate and award

Upload the evaluation report, Skip Step through negotiations, then Award Contract and pick your winner.

Expect: a contract number derived from the PR number (PR-2026-101 → CNT-2026-101), and the requisition now reads Converted to Sourcing.

Find your new contract — it'll carry a Setup badge. Click Complete contract setup and fill the five steps: terms, milestones, payment schedule, signed contract, and Ama Darko as coordinator.

Expect: a deliverable is seeded for each milestone you entered.
E2Ama — Coordinator

Upload the proof of delivery

Still as Ama, click Accept on the deliverable you just submitted.

E4James — Supervisor

Upload the invoice document first — the fields stay locked until you do. Then pick the contract, which fills in the supplier by itself, and enter the number and amount.

Expect: no payment button — only the action for the stage it's actually at. The chain cannot be skipped.

F6Ama — Coordinator

Part G — Score and close

Step 10

G1Ama, then James

Evaluate the supplier

… open the contract › Performance

Choose Final, set the five scores, name James as supervisor, submit. Set the scores below 5 if you want to see the flagging.

Expect: the score appears on the supplier's profile in Supplier Management by itself. Below 5, the supplier is automatically flagged.
G2Felix — Procurement

Try to close with work outstanding

… open the contract › Close-Out

Click Close Contract before everything is done.

Expect: refused, naming exactly what's missing — e.g. "2 invoices not yet paid: INV-…".
G3Felix — Procurement

Finish the checklist and close

Tick Procurement confirms compliance (the only manual one), generate the Certificate of Completion and Final Closure Report, then Close Contract.

Expect: status Closed. The other four checklist items were computed, not ticked by hand.
