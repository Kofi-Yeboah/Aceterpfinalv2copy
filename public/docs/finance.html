How money works at ACET

Nine flows, start to finish. Each is a short page saying what happens, why it matters, where it is done, and what changes in the system when it does.

Where this is today

Everything described here is how the module is designed to work, and the rules it applies — balancing an entry, the order of the month-end checklist, the four checks on an invoice — all genuinely work on screen.

What is not finished is the plumbing between screens. Most finance screens still keep their own copy of the data, so an entry created on one screen will not appear on another, and changes are lost when you navigate away. Demonstrate one screen at a time and it holds up; move between two and it will not.

The exception is Finance › Approvals › Procurement requests, which is fully connected — approving there really does move the requisition forward for everyone else in the system.

The nine flows

Each one is a short page. Read them in order the first time, or jump to the one you need.

Money arrives

Flow 01

A donor commits money, and it becomes a fund with a balance that budgets can be built against.

Setting the budget

Flow 02

Deciding in advance what each department and each project may spend, then watching what actually happens against it.

Writing down what happened

Flow 03

The heart of the module. Every transaction recorded twice, checked by a second person, and corrected by reversal rather than deletion.

Paying for something

Flow 04

Money leaving the organisation. Five different front doors — invoices, expenses, advances, petty cash, payroll — and one road out.

Checking the details still add up

Flow 05

The books keep one total; the detailed list sits elsewhere. Making sure the two still agree before the month is closed.

Working in more than one currency

Flow 06

Donors give in dollars, we spend in cedis. Which rate is used when, and what happens when the rate moves.

Things we buy that last

Flow 07

Spreading the cost of a vehicle or a laptop across the years it is used, and choosing the method that matches how it loses value.

Closing the month

Flow 08

The twelve-step checklist, why the order cannot be changed, and the difference between a close you can undo and one you cannot.

The reports that come out

Flow 09

Trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and closing balances — all worked out from the same entries.
