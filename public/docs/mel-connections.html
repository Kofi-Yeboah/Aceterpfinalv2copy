How M&E connects to everything else

M&E sits across the whole system rather than at the end of a chain. These are the doors between it and everything else.

ModuleWhat crossesWhy
Project ManagementThe project an indicator belongs to, and the phase, deliverable or task it is attached toAn indicator has to be measured against something real that someone is delivering
Project ManagementIndicator performance back against project deliveryA project can be on schedule and still not be achieving anything — both views are needed
FinanceGrant utilisation and funding by donorResults without spend is half the picture. Donors ask what was achieved and what it cost
CRM · Grant ManagementThe donors and agreements behind every reporting obligationThe agreement is what creates the obligation in the first place
ProcurementThe goods and services that make the activities possibleAn activity in a framework usually needs something bought before it can happen
ManagementIndicator and data quality figuresThey feed the portfolio and board reporting
The pattern worth noticing

M&E sits across everything rather than at the end of a chain. Procurement buys what the activity needs, finance pays for it and tracks the cost, projects deliver it — and M&E is the only module asking whether any of it produced the change we said it would.
