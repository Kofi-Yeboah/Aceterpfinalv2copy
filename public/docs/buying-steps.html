Buying something — the ten steps

What happens at each stage, who does it, and the exact screen and button in the system where it gets done.

What happens
Where in the system
Before you can buy
1

A supplier joins our list

They send their papers. We check they're real, current, and not on a banned list. Then they get an ID number and join the approved list.

Procurement · or the supplier

Supplier Management › Onboard Firm

Fill the form, attach each document with its expiry date, then Complete Onboarding.

Approvals › Supplier Registrations

The new supplier waits there until Procurement clicks Approve. The button refuses while a mandatory document is missing.

2

We write down what we plan to buy

Each team lists what they'll need and what it costs. Procurement checks the rules, Finance checks the money. Both must agree.

Requestor → Procurement → Finance

Purchase Plan › Departmental Plans › Add activity

Typing the cost suggests the buying method. Then Save & submit for review. Project-funded work goes under Project Plans instead.

Approvals › Departmental Plan Approval  ·  or Project Plan Approval

Two queues on each — Procurement review, then Finance verification. Open one, click Review, approve or reject with a reason. The two kinds of plan never mix.

Getting it approved
3

Someone asks to buy something

They pick the item off the plan, say which fund pays, and attach the specification or terms of reference.

Requestor

Purchase Requisitions › Add New Requisition

Then Submit Requisition. If anything's missing the button won't go through — red messages point at the exact fields.

4

The bosses say yes

Manager first, then Procurement and Finance together, then the COO if it's over $10,000. A rejection must carry a written reason.

Dept Head → Procurement + Finance → COO

Approvals › Purchase Requisitions

Approvals › Senior Management Approval

Each approver works their own queue: Approve or Reject. Rejected work comes back via Resubmit for Approval.

Finding a supplier
5

We look for someone to supply it

Small job: ask a few for prices. Medium: invite a shortlist. Big: advertise openly. The value decides.

Procurement
Happens automatically

Sourcing

The case appears on its own once the requisition is approved. Open it for a stepper of the right stages. Change Method needs a reason if it breaks the value band.

6

Suppliers send in their offers

Offers arrive sealed. Nobody at ACET can open one until the closing date has passed.

Suppliers · Procurement

Sourcing › case › Advertisement / Invite Bidders

Publish with Publish Now, or pick the shortlist and Send Invitations.

Sourcing › case › Bid Opening

Log each offer as it arrives with Record Bid Received. It gets a receipt number and a timestamp, and stays sealed.

7

We pick a winner and sign

Technical scores are settled before prices are revealed. Then the request number becomes the contract number.

Evaluation panel · Procurement

Sourcing › case › Bid Opening

In order: Close Tender → Open Technical Envelope → Open Financial Envelope. Opening early is refused.

Sourcing › case › Contract Award

Award Contract — blocked suppliers can't be picked. Then Generate Purchase Order for goods.

After the contract
8

They do the work, and we check it

The coordinator uploads the proof. Somebody else has to accept it — you can't approve your own work.

Contract Coordinator → a second reviewer

Contract Management › contract › Deliverables

Upload Deliverable, then a different person clicks Accept or Reject.

9

They send a bill, and we pay it

Four people in order. The bill must be matched to work we already accepted before it can move at all.

Coordinator → Procurement → Supervisor → Finance

Invoices  ·  or Contract Management › Invoices & Payments

Match & Review → Procurement Review → Supervisor Approval → Process Payment. Only the button for the current stage is shown.

Query sends it back to the supplier with a reason at any stage.

10

We close the job and score them

Score out of 10 on quality, timeliness, cost, rules and behaviour. Then the closing checklist and the certificate.

Coordinator + Supervisor · Procurement · Finance

Contract Management › contract › Performance

Score it, add a supervisor, submit. It lands on the supplier's profile by itself.

Contract Management › contract › Close-Out

Generate the certificate and report, then Close Contract. If anything's outstanding it refuses and names it.

↺ Back to step 1. The score from step 10 lands on the supplier's record and decides who may be invited next time. Score below 5 and they're flagged — the next award needs management approval before it can go ahead.

ACET ERP · Procurement module. All paths start from the Procurement section of the left sidebar. Switch the acting user with the name badge at the top right — the system won't let one person approve their own work.
