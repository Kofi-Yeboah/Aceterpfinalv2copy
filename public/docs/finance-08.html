Closing the month

Drawing a line under a period so the numbers stop moving and can be reported. It is a checklist, and the order is not optional.

Hard closelocked, permanently

Soft close is the safety net. While a month is soft-closed it offers both Reopen and Continue Close. Once it is hard-closed there are no buttons at all — just a padlock. That difference is deliberate: a month under review can be pulled back, a month declared final cannot.

Finance

The twelve steps, in this order

  1. Run depreciation — charge this month's wear on every asset
  2. Accrue expenses — record things we have incurred but not been invoiced for yet
  3. Revalue foreign currency — restate foreign balances at the closing rate
  4. Reconcile the detailed lists — confirm they agree with the summary totals
  5. Generate closing entries — for the accounts that reset each year
  6. Post everything outstanding — nothing approved is left unposted
  7. Produce the trial balance — check the two sides of the books still agree
  8. Review final adjustments — last corrections before the numbers freeze
  9. Close income and spending — sweep them into the year's result
  10. Move the result to reserves — the profit or loss becomes part of accumulated funds
  11. Lock the period — nothing more can be posted
  12. Produce the statements — the reports for the closed month
In the system

Finance › Period Management

Click Start Close on an open month. A progress bar shows how far through you are, and a step cannot be ticked while an earlier one is still outstanding.

2
Finance

Why that order and no other

Each step depends on the one before it. Depreciation and accruals have to be in before anything is revalued, or you are revaluing an incomplete picture. Revaluation has to be done before the detailed lists are reconciled, or they will not match. Everything has to be posted before a trial balance means anything. And the month is only locked once the numbers it is about to report have stopped moving.

Why it matters Skipping a step does not just miss that step. It quietly invalidates every step after it, and the resulting statements look completely normal.
What changes when a month is hard-closed
  • Nothing further can be posted into it — a late invoice has to go into the next month instead.
  • The statements for that month become final.
  • The decision cannot be undone.
